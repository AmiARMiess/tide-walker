Reimagined Abilities

Every spell has been transformed to match the deep sea horror theme.

Blade Fury

Transforms into a whirlpool of dark water and debris. Cyan lightning arcs through the vortex as shells and bones orbit the blade.

Healing Ward

Summons a bioluminescent coral formation instead of a ward. Pulses with healing light and emits floating plankton particles.

Blade Dance

Critical strikes trigger a splash of saltwater and a burst of bioluminescent spores at the impact location.

Omnislash

Each slash leaves a trailing ripple effect. The final strike creates a massive tidal wave visual that crashes across the battlefield.

The Legend of the Deep

"Once a captain who sailed the edges of the map, Yurnero was swallowed by a trench that had no bottom. He did not drown; he changed. Now, he walks the land carrying the crushing pressure of the deep ocean within his blade."

His movements leave puddles of saltwater that boil with unseen life. The Trench-Cleaver, forged from a rusted anchor and coral, drips with abyssal water that never dries.
